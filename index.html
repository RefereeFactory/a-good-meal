How it works

  1. You enter a London postcode; we turn it into a map location using the free OpenStreetMap Nominatim service.
  2. We search OpenStreetMap's live restaurant data (via the Overpass API) within your chosen radius — capped at 3 miles.
  3. If you picked a cuisine, we keep the closest matches first and show the distance to each.
  4. Your email and phone are saved securely so we can share future tools — search still works either way, and you control your details.

Data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Distances are straight-line estimates. Please confirm opening hours and menus with each restaurant directly.
