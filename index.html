How it works

  1. Share your details. Your email, phone and postcode are stored securely so the app maker can send you dining ideas later. Everything saves to the app's permanent cloud store.
  2. Pick a cuisine and radius. Choose what you fancy and how far you'll travel — from a 300 m stroll to a 5 km hop across the City.
  3. We search live. Your postcode is turned into a map location, then we look up real restaurants nearby from a public places directory and list their address, cuisine and contact details.
  4. You decide. Tap a phone number to call or open a website to check the menu, ratings and prices before you go.
